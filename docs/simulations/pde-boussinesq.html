Boussinesq cavity — convection onset at three Rayleigh numbers

Frozen-diffheat scenario pde-boussinesq. Full MAC + artificial-compressibility is too heavy for 60 fps JS; this page uses an honest reduced model: T field advected by a diagnostic circulation whose amplitude is set by the Ra-dependent Nu correlation. The reduced model is labelled as such.

1 · The setup

Unit square, hot bottom T=1, cold top T=0, insulated sides, Pr=0.707. Ra slider (log 1e3–2e5) with three presets. Below Ra_c=1708: pure conduction (linear profile). Above: circulation grows, plumes tilt, Nu rises. Nu from Globe–Dropkin correlation: Nu = 0.069·Ra^0.333·Pr^0.074 (3e5<Ra<7e9); laminar branch Nu = 1 + 1.44·(1−1708/Ra) below. Validated envelope: Ra ≤ 3e5 at this resolution — extrapolations flagged.
