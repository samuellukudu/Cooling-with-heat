Heat on a disc — exact Bessel eigenfunction series

Frozen-diffheat scenario pde-disc. The polar basis fits the circular domain exactly — no grid, no CFL, no numerical diffusion. The series below is evaluated live in JS.

1 · The series

u(r,θ,t) = Σm,n vm,n(r,θ)·cm,n·exp(−λm,nt) on R=1 with u=0 on the rim. Initial condition: off-centre Gaussian bump at r=0.45, amplitude 1+0.3·cos θ. First three axisymmetric modes (J₀ zeros 2.4048, 5.5201, 8.6537) plus the cos θ mode (J₁ zero 3.8317). Coefficients c = [0.62, −0.21, 0.08] (axisymmetric) and 0.15 (cos θ term) — approximate projections of the bump.

peak now–dominant λ = 2.4048² = 5.783decay rate = λ₁ (slowest mode)

The peak decays at rate λ₁ = 5.783 — the slowest Bessel eigenvalue. Asymmetry (cos θ) decays faster (λ = 3.8317² = 14.68), so the field becomes axisymmetric as t grows.
