Absorption chiller — liquid sorption turns 85 °C heat into 7 °C cold

Model: single-effect LiBr/H₂O-class lumped cycle (same form as harness.physics.absorption): COP_ideal = ((T_gen−T_cond)/T_gen)·(T_evap/(T_cond−T_evap)), derated by pair effectiveness ε; capacity from concentration spread Δx = clip(a·(T_gen−T_cond) − b·(T_cond−T_evap), 0, Δx_max). Calibration anchor: 7/30/85 °C LiBr-H₂O → COP ≈ 0.70 (ideal 1.87 × ε 0.37).

1 · The loop (four vessels + solution circuit)

Generator (85 °C waste/solar heat in): boils water vapour out of the LiBr solution — solution concentrates.

Condenser (30 °C tower water): vapour condenses, heat rejected. Evaporator (7 °C chilled water): liquid flashes at low pressure, absorbing the building's heat — this is the cooling. Absorber (30 °C): concentrated solution re-absorbs the vapour (more heat rejected), diluted solution pumps back to the generator through the recuperating heat exchanger. No compressor — the thermal compressor is the solution circuit driven by Q_gen.
