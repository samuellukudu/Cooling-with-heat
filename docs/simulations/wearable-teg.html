Wearable TEG — body heat → electricity via Seebeck effect

From intro-research: flexible TEGs integrated into fabrics convert body heat (warm side) and ambient air (cool side) into power. Microwatts per cm² — manage cooling-element power carefully.

1 · The device

A flexible thermoelectric generator laminated into a garment. Body side at T_hot (skin ~33 °C), ambient side at T_cold (air, slider). The Seebeck voltage V = S·ΔT drives current through a load. Power P = V²/(R_int + R_load). Maximum power transfer when R_load = R_int. ZT = S²T_avg/(R·K) sets the efficiency ceiling.
