Human comfort / HVAC — solar heat in, 10 °C chilled water out

Application human: T_evap 10 °C · T_cond 35 °C · T_des 80 °C · half-cycle 600 s · fluid 75–95 °C · COP weight 0.40 (efficiency case)

1 · The plumbing (this building only)

Heat in: flat-plate solar collectors (75–95 °C, hour slider below) heat water that regenerates the bed at 80 °C. Cooling out: the evaporator at 10 °C chills a fan-coil water loop that holds rooms near 24 °C / 55% RH. Rejected: adsorption + condensation heat at 35 °C to a rooftop dry cooler.

Long 600 s halves: the bed is thick (3.0 mm) and slow — fine here, because buildings reward COP over power density. The day trace shows the point: cooling follows the sun with a thermal lag.
