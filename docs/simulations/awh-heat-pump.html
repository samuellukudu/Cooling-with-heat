Air-water heat (AWH) — adsorption heat pump

From anchor notes: CAU-10-H and AlPO-18 are common heat-pump / AWH candidates. The adsorption heat pump uses the same D–A cycle as cooling but runs "backwards": low-grade heat drives desorption, and the adsorption step delivers high-grade heat. COP_heat = Q_delivered / Q_regeneration.

1 · The cycle

Evaporator at T_evap (water source, e.g. 10 °C), condenser at T_cond (heat delivery, e.g. 50 °C), regenerator at T_reg (waste heat, e.g. 80 °C). The bed adsorbs at T_evap (heat released to the water source), is heated to T_reg (desorption, heat input), then condenses at T_cond (heat delivered to the load). COP_heat = (Q_ads + Q_cond) / Q_reg. The same D–A physics as the cooling cycle, but the objective is heat delivery, not cooling.

COP_heat > 1 means the pump delivers more heat than the regeneration input — the difference comes from the evaporator (water source). This is the heat-pump analogue of the cooling cycle.
