Solar thermal cooling — flat-plate collector → adsorption chiller

Standalone application: solar collectors drive the bed; the evaporator makes chilled water. The collector temperature follows the sun; the bed desorbs when the collector is hot enough. This is the solar-only version of the human-hvac-solar page, with the full day trace and the collector efficiency curve.

1 · The system

Flat-plate solar collectors (efficiency η = 0.75 − 0.005·(T_coll − T_amb)) heat a water loop. The loop drives the bed at T_des = T_coll − 10 (heat exchanger loss). The evaporator at 10 °C makes chilled water for fan-coils. The condenser at 35 °C rejects to a dry cooler. The day trace shows collector temperature, cooling power, and COP over 6 h to 20 h.
