TEC wearable cooling — Peltier module in a garment

From intro-research: active cooling textiles incorporate TEC modules or miniature fans directly. The TEC is the Peltier side: electricity → cooling. Distinct from the frozen pde-thermoelectric (module physics); this is the garment integration.

1 · The module

A flexible Peltier module laminated into a cooling vest. Cold side against skin (T_c), hot side to ambient (T_h). Q_c = S·T_c·I − ½I²R − K·ΔT. COP = Q_c/W. The vest must reject Q_h = Q_c + W on the hot side — heat sink or fabric spreader. Power comes from a battery or a TEG/TGC harvester (see wearable-teg / wearable-tgc).
