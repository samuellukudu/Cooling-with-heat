Forced convection — channel flow over a heated chip

Frozen-diffheat scenario pde-forced-conv, live JS solver. One-way coupling: flow prescribed, not solved.

1 · The setup

Channel [0,4]×[0,1], uniform horizontal flow u=(U,0). Cold Dirichlet inlet/outlet (T=0), insulated top/bottom. Gaussian chip source at (2.0, 0.15). dT/dt = α∇²T − U·dT/dx + S(x,y). First-order upwind advection + 5-point diffusion, explicit Euler. Defaults: U=1.0, α=0.01 (frozen DEFAULT_ALPHA), grid 72×18 live.
