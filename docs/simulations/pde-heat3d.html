Heat 3D — cube: three mid-planes animated together

Frozen-diffheat scenario pde-heat3d, live JS solver. N=16 per side (20³ in the frozen run — live JS stays at 16³ for 60 fps).

1 · The setup

Unit cube, initially 0 °C. Left face (x=0) held at 100 °C, right face (x=1) at 0 °C, all other faces insulated. Explicit 7-point Laplacian on a 16³ grid. Three mid-planes (xy at mid-z, xz at mid-y, yz at mid-x) are shown side by side — the yz plane is the one driven by the boundary condition; the other two show the same gradient extrapolated through the insulated directions.
