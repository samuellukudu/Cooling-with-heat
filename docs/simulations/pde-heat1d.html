Heat 1D — transient slab: Dirichlet ends, approach to linear steady

Frozen-diffheat scenario pde-heat1d, reimplemented as a live JS finite-difference solver — no embedded replay.

1 · The setup

Slab L=1, both ends held at fixed temperature: T(0)=1, T(1)=0. Interior starts at 0. Explicit FTCS: T[i] += dt·α·(T[i−1]−2T[i]+T[i+1])/dx² with N=64 and dt from the CFL limit (dt = 0.9·dx²/(2α)). The field relaxes to the analytic linear steady state 1−x at rate λ₁ = π²α — the slowest eigenmode of the 1-D Laplacian. Heat enters through the hot end and leaves through the cold end; flux arrows below are ∝ the live boundary fluxes −α·∂T/∂x.
