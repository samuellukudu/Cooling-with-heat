Application datacenter_dynamic: same setpoints as datacenter + source schedule 57.5 + 12.5·sin(2πt/4h) °C (45–70 °C)

1 · What moves (only this page)

The IT load swings on a 4-hour period, so the warm-water loop swings 45 → 70 → 45 °C. The bed's desorption wall follows it; the adsorb wall stays at 35 °C and the evaporator at 16 °C. Scrub the day: Q_in and cooling swell at the 70 °C crest and starve in the 45 °C trough — and the bed temperature lags the loop because 2 mm of adsorbent cannot heat instantly.
